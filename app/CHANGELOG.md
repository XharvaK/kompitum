# Kompitum changelog

## [0.2.0] - 2026-11-08
<!-- spotlight -->

### New
- **Kompitum is its own app.** It started from herdr, and now it has its own name, its own mark and its own core, `kom`.
- **Desk puts your agents beside your work.** The Board lists every worker: what waits for you, the queue, and what each row can do. The Line shows the notes your agents leave each other, newest at the bottom.
- **The Lead switch.** Pick which orchestrator leads, and open the other one's chat in one click. A hand-off is a note in the vault, never a lock.
- **One look, fewer buttons.** Kompitum opens on Desk in ink, or paper in light mode. Add machine is in Settings › Machines.
- **Nothing leaves your computer.** Kompitum sends no usage data. Send feedback opens an issue on GitHub, filled in, for you to read before you submit it.
- Updates come from Kompitum's own GitHub.
