# Kompitum changelog

## [0.2.0] - 2026-11-08
<!-- spotlight -->

### New
- **One conversation with your lead.** You talk to it in one place. It plans the work and hands parts of it to workers.
- **Cards you answer in place.** Permissions, plans and questions arrive as cards in the composer.
- **Workers in the background.** They run without windows. The bell shows what waits for you.
- **Yours alone.** Kompitum sends nothing about you anywhere. Its counts stay on this computer.
